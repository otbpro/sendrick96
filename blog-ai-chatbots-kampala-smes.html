How AI Chatbots Cut Support Time in Half for Kampala SMEs

Practical lessons from real customer support chatbots I’ve deployed for small teams in Kampala — including what worked, what failed, and how to start safely.

1. The before-and-after picture

Most SMEs I speak to have the same pattern: WhatsApp and calls running 24/7, the same 15–20 questions repeated all day, and staff who spend more time replying than selling. After rolling out chatbots on web and WhatsApp, 40–60% of those questions get answered instantly — and the support team only jumps in for complex cases.

2. Where chatbots make the biggest impact

  • Answering repetitive FAQs (prices, delivery areas, opening hours, product details).
  • Qualifying leads before a human joins (budget, timeline, business size).
  • Capturing contact details 24/7 and pushing them into simple CRMs or Google Sheets.

3. Recommended tools and starting stack

For most Kampala SMEs, I recommend starting with a WhatsApp chatbot (for example via approved WhatsApp Business API providers) plus a simple web widget. The bot can connect to your existing tools — email, spreadsheets or CRM — without forcing a full system change.
