How AI Chatbots Reduce Repetitive Support Work for Kampala SMEs

A grounded guide to where chatbots help, how to keep answers useful, and when a real person should take over. Start with one repetitive customer task, then measure whether it improves service.

1. Start with a repeatable customer question

Review recent chats and support calls to find a small set of frequent, predictable questions: delivery areas, opening hours, product availability, or how to place an order. Write down the approved answer for each and note where the answer depends on current stock, price, or location. Automate only the questions your team can answer consistently.

2. Design a useful conversation—not a maze

  • Offer a few clear starting choices and let customers ask a question in their own words.
  • Ask only for details needed to help, such as a delivery area or preferred callback time.
  • Show a clear path to a staff member whenever the bot is unsure or the request is sensitive.

3. Connect it to trusted, current information

Start with the channel customers already use—your website, WhatsApp Business, or both—and confirm the provider is authorized for the channel and works with your existing support process. Keep the bot's source content short, reviewed, and easy to update. Avoid connecting it to private customer records unless you have a clear business need and appropriate access controls.

4. Test, measure, and improve

Before launch, test common questions, misspellings, out-of-scope requests, and requests to speak with a person. Review a sample of conversations each week. Track successful resolutions, handoffs, unanswered questions, and customer feedback; don't judge success by chat volume alone.
