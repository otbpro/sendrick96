How to Choose Your First Digital Project (Without Wasting Budget)

A simple decision framework I use with founders to pick the highest-impact starting point: website, app, AI or marketing.

1. Clarify your main constraint

  • If you lack credibility: start with a strong website and case studies.
  • If you have demand but can’t handle it: invest in automation and operations tools.
  • If people don’t know you exist: prioritise marketing and funnels.

2. Score options on impact vs. difficulty

I give each idea a 1–5 score for expected revenue impact, speed to launch and complexity. The best first project is usually high-impact, medium-effort, not “perfect but impossible”.
