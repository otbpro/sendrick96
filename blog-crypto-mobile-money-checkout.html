Adding Crypto and Mobile Money to Your Checkout Safely

A practical overview of how East African brands can offer card, mobile money and crypto payments without breaking UX — or security.

1. Design a simple, trusted checkout first

Before adding more payment options, fix the basics: clear order summary, visible contact options, SSL certificate, and minimal form steps. Every new method should feel like an addition, not a separate experience.

2. Work with licensed, battle-tested providers

In East Africa, that usually means payment gateways that already support cards and mobile money, and partner with crypto processors rather than home-grown wallets on your server.
