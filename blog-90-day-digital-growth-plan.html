Inside a 90-Day Digital Growth Plan for a Kampala Retail Brand

A practical framework for a retail team that wants more online sales without scattering effort across disconnected channels. Adapt the timeline to your capacity and use the same few metrics throughout the quarter.

Phase 1 (Weeks 1–2): Audit & quick fixes

  • Record a baseline: orders, qualified enquiries, conversion rate, average order value, and acquisition cost where available.
  • Walk through the purchase journey on a phone; fix confusing navigation, missing product details, and broken checkout steps.
  • Choose one primary outcome and a small set of supporting measures. Assign an owner to review them weekly.

Phase 2 (Weeks 3–6): Content & campaign engine

Build a repeatable content calendar around customer questions, product use, and proof such as reviews or demonstrations. Choose the channels your audience actually uses; connect each campaign to a focused landing page with a clear offer and a trackable call to action.

Phase 3 (Weeks 7–12): Scale what works

Compare results against the baseline and account for stock, seasonality, and campaign spend. Improve strong campaigns in measured steps; pause or revise weak ones after enough data to make a fair comparison. Follow up on abandoned carts or enquiries only with appropriate consent and a useful message.

4. A simple weekly review

Keep a short record of what shipped, what customers did, and what you will change next week. If traffic rises but sales do not, inspect product pages, delivery information, price clarity, and checkout friction before buying more reach. If orders grow but margins fall, revisit offer costs and fulfilment capacity.
